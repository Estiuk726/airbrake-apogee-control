Airbrake Apogee Controller

Each flight runs the full guidance chain: noisy barometer and accelerometer, a Kalman filter, an onboard apogee predictor and a flap controller. The rocket panel sets the nominal design the flight computer believes in. The real-world sliders change the actual flight without telling the computer.
