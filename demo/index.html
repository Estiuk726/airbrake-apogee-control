Motor: any RASP .eng file, the standard format on thrustcurve.org and in OpenRocket. Propellant mass is read from the file.

Drag: a CSV with Mach and drag coefficient. From OpenRocket: run a simulation, choose Export data, tick Mach number and Drag coefficient. From RocketPy: the two-column mach,cd drag file. Readings are averaged into Mach bins.

2-D point-mass simulation: off the rail the rocket aligns instantly with the relative wind, a worst case for weathercocking; no gusts.
